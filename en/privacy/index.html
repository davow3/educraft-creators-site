Privacy policy

Established: July 8, 2026 · Updated: October 31, 2026

Apps covered

This policy applies to Math Quest Masters, Math Defender, Kazu Tap, Math Fight, and Kaba's Sleepy Watermelon, including apps being prepared for release.

Information used by the apps

The apps do not ask users to enter their real name, email address, or contacts. Math Fight uses the anonymous profile and battle information described below. Information processed by advertising services is explained under Advertising.

In Kaba's Sleepy Watermelon, photos selected from the device, their references, custom place names, and character lines are used only on the device. They are not sent to the developer's server or third parties.

Information stored on the device

Depending on the app, the device may store save/profile names, score and answer histories, learning progress, chapter and mission achievements, sound and language settings, policy acknowledgement, purchase/unlock status, and locally selected photo references or custom text.

Math Fight may also store a server-issued anonymous player ID and credentials, a selected battle name, optional country/region, character, accessories, and collection progress. Device data is not sent to external services except as explained for online play and purchases below.

Network services and online battles

Math Fight uses Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects for online battle relay and anonymous battle records, Google AdMob for ads, and Google Play Billing for purchases and restoration. The apps do not add their own analytics or external accounts based on an email address.

During online play, temporary room codes or matchmaking information, grade/rank, character number, display language, question number, selected answers, response time, HP, combos, and connection status are transmitted and processed. A battle name chosen from preset words, an optional country/region, and equipped accessories may also be shared with the opponent. No freely entered name, chat, real name, contacts, advertising ID, or device ID is sent to the battle server. Country/region is selected by the user, not inferred from location or IP address.

For random battles, the server stores an anonymous player ID, grade, match count, wins, win rate, rating, battle name, optional country/region, and the last character and accessories, for features such as grade-based rankings. Friend-battle results are not added to these rankings. An automated opponent may participate if a human opponent is unavailable; those results are kept separate from human-player ranking records.

Battle rooms expire 20 minutes after the last battle, and unmatched matchmaking entries expire after two minutes. Anonymous profiles and random-battle records are retained until a deletion request is successfully processed. Cloudflare may temporarily process network information such as IP addresses to establish connections and maintain security. The apps do not keep their own analytics or communication logs.

Purchases

Apps with paid features may include internet and network-state permissions required by Google Play Billing for purchases and restoration. These permissions are not used to send learning scores, photos, custom place names, or character lines to the developer. The developer does not receive payment card numbers.

Advertising

Math Fight displays Google AdMob banner ads with child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings, a general-audience content rating limit, and non-personalized ad requests. The Android advertising ID permission is removed from the app.

For ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, the advertising SDK may transmit and process IP addresses, app/ad interactions, diagnostics, and identifier information. An approximate region may be inferred from an IP address. The app does not request GPS or other location permissions.

After an ad-off purchase or restoration, the app stops loading and displaying ads. Google Play Billing processes the purchase. Math Quest Masters may show a More EduCraft apps menu; an external Google Play page or browser opens only after confirmation.

Delete Math Fight data

To delete the anonymous player ID, random-battle records, and server profile:

  1. Open Math Fight while connected to the internet.
  2. Open Settings from the home screen and choose the option to reset wins/losses and online records. Confirm the reset.
  3. The server data is deleted when the server successfully processes the request. If the connection fails, reconnect and try again.

Local learning histories, scores, stamps, and review records can be deleted in supported apps' settings. A normal Math Fight record reset keeps the on-device accessory collection and ad-off purchase status. Starting random matchmaking again after deletion creates a new anonymous ID.

Google Play purchase records and data handled by AdMob or other external services follow those services' retention and deletion policies. Temporary rooms and matchmaking entries expire as described above.

Website language preference

The main website entry uses the browser's preferred languages to open a supported language page. Language-specific URLs keep the language you opened. A manually chosen language is stored in this browser's local storage and can be changed with the language links or cleared by deleting site data. The website does not use IP geolocation or add analytics for this feature. Google Play links request the page language; prices and availability remain controlled by Google Play and your account.

Music and sound effects

The apps use music and sound effects from OtoLogic. Sources and licenses are listed in the in-app credits.

Contact

Use App support on each app's Google Play listing to contact us.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as app features, release availability, applicable requirements, or store policies change.
