How to play

  1. Choose a level and chapter that suit the learner.
  2. Read the question, choose an answer, and advance through the battle.
  3. Use the game's learning and review features to revisit topics, then continue your adventure.

Questions from parents

Who is this game for?

Children learning elementary-school math and families looking for a playful way to practice. The grade labels are based on Japanese grades 1–6, so choose by the actual topics rather than age alone.

Which languages can I use?

The interface supports Japanese, English, Spanish, Indonesian, and Korean. The learning sequence follows Japanese elementary-school math topics.

Is this the same as Math Fight?

Math Quest Masters focuses on a single-player RPG adventure. Math Fight focuses on math battles with family, friends, and online opponents.

Where can I check price and purchase details?

Use the Google Play link for current availability and pricing. Content unlocks and purchase details are explained in the app before purchase.
