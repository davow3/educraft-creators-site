Math Fight

One phone. Two players. A math showdown.

Face each other across one Android phone for a family math battle. Children and adults can choose their own grade and level. You can also battle a friend remotely or find an online opponent.

For families and friends who want to practice math together. Adjust each player's level to make a shared challenge more enjoyable.

Math Fight displays banner ads. An in-app purchase can turn ads off; check Google Play and the app for current pricing and details.

How to play

  1. Choose local two-player or online play.
  2. Set your grade, level, and character, then start the battle.
  3. Answer math questions and use the game's battle actions to challenge your opponent.

Coming in a future update

We are preparing offline CPU battles, character accessories, and a grade-based top-three display. Some screenshots and videos show these upcoming features; availability depends on the version distributed by Google Play.

Questions from parents

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Local two-player mode lets two players face each other across a single Android phone.

Can a parent and child use different levels?

Yes. Each player can choose their own grade and level. Adjust the settings to fit each learner.

Do I need an internet connection?

Online battles need an internet connection. An offline CPU mode is being prepared for a future update; check the version and information on Google Play.

Which languages are supported?

Japanese, English, Spanish, Indonesian, and Korean are available in the app.

Does the game have ads or purchases?

The game displays banner ads and offers an in-app purchase to turn ads off. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.
