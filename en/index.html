Small ideas, made into playable worlds

EduCraft Creators is an educational app brand by EduCraft Studio. Our games grew from learning and creative activities at home, with original character ideas and illustrations by akimichi.

We build and improve small games that give children a reason to try, think, and try again. Math Quest Masters and Math Fight are available on Google Play; our other projects are still in development.
